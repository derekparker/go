=== Top-line finding ===
Despite getcpu being called 479,662 times in this one run (per strace),
its REAL cost (perf sampling, uninflated) is negligible: 0.01% of
cycles, and the entire numa-syscall/routing-decision code path sums to
just 0.050% of cycles. getcpu itself is a fast syscall (~50-200ns
native); strace's per-call ptrace-trap overhead inflates its apparent
share by roughly three orders of magnitude relative to its real cost.

The CPU-time cost is NOT syscall-bound. It IS substantially in-runtime:
spanSet/mcentral/sweep-path code (the per-node routing/storage mechanism
itself, not the getcpu-based node-decision that feeds it) sums to 5.29%
of total cycles -- over 100x the syscall path's share. This directly
matches Task 9's own carried-forward ruling (4): "sweep-path 8x pop cost
accepted (constant-factor, Task 11 measures)" -- (*spanSet).pop alone
(2.28%) is the single largest NUMA-attributable symbol in this profile,
consistent with the 8x-more-spanSets-to-check-per-refill mechanism that
ruling named. This is attribution evidence from one run, not a
statistical claim -- but it is a clean, well-quantified signal pointing
at a specific, previously-named mechanism, not a diffuse or ambiguous
one.
