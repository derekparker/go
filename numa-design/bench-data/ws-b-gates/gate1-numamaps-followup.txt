Both samples corroborate Gate 1's span-refill-based routing-correctness
finding via an independent measurement channel (physical page residency,
not the runtime's own refill counters). The node0 sample's 4.65% residual
on node1 is consistent with the same pre-topology-discovery fallback

counting all VMAs (not only the per-node heap streams) -- unrelated
regions (thread stacks, kernel-adjacent allocations) are not routed by
this mechanism at all and can land on either node.
